8. Contacting the Developers

The ActionApps were developed by the Association for Progressive Communications, https://www.apc.org/. They are released as open source; development happens at https://github.com/apc-aa/ActionApps (source code, history, issues). The SourceForge project https://sourceforge.net/projects/apc-aa/ keeps the SVN mirror and the archived trackers.

Questions and bug reports: https://github.com/apc-aa/ActionApps/issues, or https://actionapps.org/.
